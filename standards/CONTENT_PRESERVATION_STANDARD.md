# Content Preservation Standard v3.4

## Core rule

An existing article is a content asset. Optimization must preserve useful information before it changes wording, structure, length, or style.

## Protected source assets

Default-protect the following unless a documented reason says otherwise:

- Search-answer modules: Quick Answer, decision trees, FAQ, Verdict.
- Tables, comparison matrices, checklists, and workflows.
- Detailed procedural steps and scenario guidance.
- Verified data, sources, authority references, technical limits, and EEAT language.
- Examples, cases, and user decision information.
- Product recommendation, comparison, product workflow, proof, limitation, and CTA assets.
- Effective original images, captions, Alt text, and visual explanations.

## Allowed actions

| Action | Meaning | Required note |
|---|---|---|
| Keep | Preserve substantially unchanged. | Why it remains useful. |
| Merge | Combine genuine duplicates. | Source locations and retained details. |
| Refine | Improve clarity, accuracy, or scanability. | What changed and why. |
| Relocate | Move to a more logical decision point. | Original and new location. |
| Replace with equivalent | Substitute only when the replacement preserves the same decision/evidence value. | What equivalent value remains. |
| Delete | Remove only low-value content. | Exact reason: repeat, empty, unsupported, stale, or irrelevant. |

## Rewrite boundary rule

- Review, optimize, refresh, improve SEO, and restructure mean **do not rewrite from scratch**.
- A full rewrite requires explicit user authorization.
- Do not convert a complete article into an outline, framework, checklist, or lightweight summary.

## Retention thresholds

- Count the source article before editing.
- Default expectation: retain 80–90% of effective information value.
- If prose word count drops by more than 20%, document why and recheck every lost source asset. Any retention below 80% needs explicit user authorization.
- If the revised article is materially shorter because it removed evidence, tables, cases, workflows, or product conversion rather than true duplication, it fails.
- A user may explicitly authorize a short version; record that authorization in the delivery contract.

## Regression review

Before final delivery, compare source and revised article for:

- Word-count change.
- Tables and their decision value.
- Procedures and examples.
- Evidence and sources.
- Product content and CTA.
- FAQ and Verdict coverage.
- Original useful visuals.

Any unexpected loss is a release blocker.
